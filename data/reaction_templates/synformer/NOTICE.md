# SynFormer reaction templates

114 unique forward SMARTS from the SynFormer comprehensive library, representing
all 115 original lines (R033 duplicates R032). Ten SMARTS have local repairs; see
[SMARTS_REPAIRS.md](../SMARTS_REPAIRS.md). IDs keep the
original zero-based line numbers; the duplicate R033 is not stored.
