# PaRoutes reaction templates

176 forward rules selected and inverted from atom-mapped patent-derived
retrosynthetic templates in PaRoutes 2.0.
One rule has a local SMARTS repair; see [SMARTS_REPAIRS.md](../SMARTS_REPAIRS.md).

Samuel Genheden and Esben Bjerrum, *PaRoutes: towards a framework for benchmarking
retrosynthesis route predictions*, Digital Discovery (2022), DOI
https://doi.org/10.1039/D2DD00015F.
PaRoutes 2.0 dataset, Samuel Genheden: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7341155.
